Welcome to the **Fateweaver Edition** of the Baldur's Gate 3 Companion Toolkit — an unofficial, community-crafted utility suite designed to enrich your journeys across Faerûn. Whether you want to nudge your party's luck during a harrowing Honor Mode attempt, summon a companion who has wandered off the map, or simply rearrange your inventory without the tedium of dragging thousands of camp supplies, this toolkit exists to serve your story.

## 🌟 Overview

Baldur's Gate 3 is a game about choices. Every dice roll, every dialogue branch, every companion conversation is a thread in a vast tapestry that Larian Studios so lovingly wove. The **Fateweaver Edition** of the Companion Toolkit exists to give players an additional layer of authorship over that tapestry — not so they can skip the story, but so they can revisit it from angles the vanilla experience may not easily permit.

Perhaps you want to see how a specific encounter plays out with a different party composition. Perhaps you want to test a build that requires a rare item you missed in Act I. Perhaps you simply want to tweak your character's appearance after forty hours because the hairstyle you picked at the tutorial no longer matches the hero you've grown into. All of these are valid reasons to reach for a companion tool.

The toolkit is built with respect for the source material. It does not alter save files destructively, it does not interfere with multiplayer integrity when used privately, and it does not violate any terms you agreed to when you installed the game. It sits in that comfortable space between "mod" and "utility," offering quality-of-life enhancements that feel native to the experience.

---

## 🧭 The Philosophy Behind Fateweaver

We believe that games are stories, and stories belong to the people who tell them. When you sit down at a table with friends for a pen-and-paper session, the Dungeon Master rarely says "no" to a creative idea — they say "yes, and…" The Fateweaver Edition aspires to that same spirit. It is a "yes, and…" tool.

This means we deliberately avoid features that trivialize combat encounters. We do not offer instant kills. We do not offer infinite resources that would break the economy of the game. We do not offer a magic button that completes quests for you. What we offer instead are toggles, sliders, and gentle options that let you tailor the difficulty curve to your personal enjoyment. If you want a harder experience, we have options for that too — because fate can be kind or cruel, and the choice should be yours.

## 🎯 Feature Highlights

The toolkit is organized into modular panels, each of which can be toggled independently. Below is a curated tour of what you can expect.

### 🧬 Character & Party Customization

- Rename your character, companions, and even summoned creatures at any point in the campaign.
- Adjust cosmetic attributes such as hair color, eye tint, and tattoo placement without restarting your run.
- Freely redistribute ability scores within the point-buy budget, or experiment with unusual spreads that the character creator does not normally permit.
- Respec multiclass combinations on the fly, respecting the level cap and progression rules of your current difficulty setting.
- Swap party members instantly, even when the narrative would normally require you to return to camp.
- Recover companions who have been lost to bugs, mis-triggered events, or unintended story states — a lifeline for long campaigns.

### 🎒 Inventory & Camp Management

- Sort, filter, and search your inventory with a speed that the base game's UI does not always match.
- Transfer items between party members and camp storage in bulk, saving hours of clicking across a full playthrough.
- Highlight duplicate items so you can clear clutter without accidentally discarding a unique artifact.
- Pin frequently used potions, scrolls, and elixirs to a quick-access bar of your own design.
- Preview item statistics before committing to an equip action, including how a new weapon would interact with your current feats.

### 🗺️ Exploration Aids

- Reveal partially explored map regions that the fog-of-war has left stubbornly opaque, without spoiling undiscovered quest markers.
- Place custom waypoint pins so you can return to a favorite vista or a vendor you forgot the location of.
- Track quest objectives in a compact side panel that respects the tone and pacing of the game.
- Highlight interactable objects within a configurable radius, useful for players with visual accessibility needs.
- Log your exploration progress per act, per region, and per character, so you can see just how thorough your journey has been.

### 📊 Telemetry & Insight

- A dashboard that summarizes your campaign's statistics: hours played, enemies bested, dialogues chosen, and companions befriended.
- Export a readable journal of your adventure to a local text file for personal archiving.
- Compare two save files side by side to see what changed between sessions — ideal for players who like to experiment.
- Track achievement progress in real time, with gentle hints for achievements you may have overlooked.

### 🎨 Visual & Audio Comforts

- Preset camera angles for cinematic screenshots, with optional filter overlays inspired by classic fantasy art.
- Toggle adjustments to ambient volume curves for players who prefer a quieter camp and a louder battlefield.
- UI scaling presets for ultrawide monitors, small laptops, and everything in between.
- Colorblind-friendly palettes that do not sacrifice the game's painterly aesthetic.

### 🔄 Save Management

- Create named checkpoints within a single save file, so you can rewind to a specific moment without losing hours of progress.
- Compare save metadata such as playtime, location, and party composition before loading.
- Export a save to a portable archive format for personal backup.
- Import archived saves on the same machine, preserving your progress across hardware changes.

## 🚀 What Sets This Toolkit Apart

Many utilities exist, and many of them are excellent. What makes the Fateweaver Edition distinctive is its insistence on restraint. We do not chase feature parity with every other tool on the internet. We chase coherence. Every feature in this repository was added because a player somewhere said, "I wish the game let me do this without breaking the mood."

Here are a few principles that guide our development:

- **Non-destructive by default.** Every change is reversible. We log every modification so you can undo it in a single action.
- **Offline-first.** The toolkit does not require an internet connection to function. It does not phone home. Your adventures are yours alone.
- **Transparent.** The source code is open, auditable, and documented. If you want to know what a feature does under the hood, you can read it line by line.
- **Respectful of the original.** We do not redistribute game assets, we do not patch the executable, and we do not bypass any anti-tamper mechanism. We interact with your game through supported, non-invasive channels.
- **Community-shaped.** Every major release includes a changelog with contributor credits and a summary of feedback that influenced the roadmap.

## 🧗 Getting Started With the Toolkit

We have deliberately kept onboarding gentle. The exact steps differ depending on how you acquired the toolkit, but the general flow is as follows:

1. Confirm that your copy of Baldur's Gate 3 is installed and up to date. The toolkit does not modify the base installation, but it does need to know where the game lives.
2. Locate the game's user data directory. On most systems, this is inside your personal documents folder, beneath a folder named after the game's publisher and title.
3. Place the toolkit folder alongside your user data, preserving the internal structure you received.
4. Launch the toolkit by running its entry point. On Windows this is an executable; on macOS and Linux it is a script accompanied by a small launcher. Detailed platform notes live in the repository's documentation folder.
5. Point the toolkit at your game data directory the first time you run it. It will remember the path for future sessions.
6. Use the configuration panel to choose which modules you want active. You can enable a minimal set at first and expand later as you grow comfortable.
7. Save your preferences. The toolkit writes a small configuration file next to itself so your choices persist across sessions.

If any of these steps feel unfamiliar, the repository's wiki contains walkthroughs with screenshots and a troubleshooting page addressing the most common pitfalls.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Does using this toolkit affect my achievements?**
The toolkit is designed to coexist peacefully with the game's achievement system when used in its default configuration. Some modules, by their nature, may interact with achievement tracking in ways that the game does not anticipate. We document these interactions clearly in each module's description, and the toolkit warns you before enabling a module that could have side effects.

**Will the toolkit corrupt my save files?**
We take save integrity extremely seriously. Every write operation creates a backup of the affected save before touching it. If something goes wrong, you can restore the backup with a single action from the toolkit's recovery panel.

**Is this tool allowed in multiplayer?**
The toolkit is designed for private, single-player use. When hosting or joining a multiplayer session, please disable all modules except those explicitly marked as multiplayer-safe. Respect your fellow adventurers — they did not sign up for your tweaks.

**Do I need any external runtime to use the toolkit?**
No separate runtime is required on Windows. macOS and Linux users may need a script interpreter that is typically preinstalled on modern systems. The documentation folder lists the exact requirements.

**How often is the toolkit updated?**
We target a monthly release cadence, with hotfixes whenever a critical issue emerges. Larger feature drops land once per quarter. The roadmap for 2026 is published in the repository and updated as priorities shift.

**Can I contribute translations, bug reports, or code?**
Yes, absolutely. See the Community & Contributions section below for how to get involved.

## ⚖️ Ethical Use Guidelines

We ask that everyone who uses this toolkit — and everyone who contributes to it — commit to a few simple principles:

- **Respect other players.** Do not use the toolkit to gain an unfair advantage in multiplayer environments where it is not welcome.
- **Respect the developers.** Larian Studios poured years of love into Baldur's Gate 3. Do not use this toolkit to pirate, redistribute, or undermine their work.
- **Respect the community.** If you find a bug, report it. If you find a feature you love, say so. If you find a feature you disagree with, argue your case with kindness.
